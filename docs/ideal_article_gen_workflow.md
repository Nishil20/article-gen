# what should be the workflow for the AI article gen tool

The ideal workflow for an AI article generation tool—especially for SEO content—should be a structured, multi-step process that incorporates both AI automation and targeted human review. This ensures that your articles are not only AI-generated efficiently but are also optimized for relevance, quality, and ranking performance. Here’s a generalized step-by-step SEO article generation workflow, mixing best-of-breed tools at every phase:[^1][^2][^3][^4]

***

### SEO Article Generation Workflow

#### 1. Keyword Research \& Clustering

- **Tool(s):** NeuronWriter, Outranking, Semrush, Frase
- Gather 5–10 primary keyword variations and 8–12 long-tail keywords, grouped by search intent (informational, commercial, transactional).
- **Human Review:** Select impactful keywords, validate intent alignment.[^3][^5][^6]


#### 2. Content Brief Creation

- **Tool(s):** Frase, Clearscope, Outranking, MarketMuse
- Generate a detailed brief (target keywords, SEO title/meta, outline structure, competitor notes).
- **Human Review:** Ensure brief covers user intent and brand voice.[^2][^7]


#### 3. Outline Expansion

- **Tool(s):** ChatGPT, Copy.ai, Jasper, Frase
- Expand the outline into detailed H1/H2/H3 headings with section-specific talking points.
- **Human Review:** Tweak for logical flow and comprehensive coverage.[^8][^9]


#### 4. Draft Generation (AI Writing)

- **Tool(s):** Surfer AI, Frase, Writesonic, Jasper, Copy.ai
- Use AI to generate a full-length draft article directly from the outlined structure, ensuring in-context keyword placement.
- **Human Review:** Read for tone, accuracy, and alignment with brand standards.[^10][^11][^12]


#### 5. SEO Optimization \& Editing

- **Tool(s):** Surfer SEO, NeuronWriter, Clearscope, Rank Math, AIOSEO
- Refine draft for keyword frequency, heading structure, readability, and internal linking.
- **Human Review:** Polish prose, validate natural keyword use and fix any factual/copy errors.[^5][^13][^14]


#### 6. Meta Tags, Schema \& FAQ

- **Tool(s):** Rank Math, GetGenie, Writesonic, Frase
- Generate meta title, meta description, article schema, and FAQ schema markup.
- **Human Review:** Verify meta tags are within character limits and are compelling.[^15][^16]


#### 7. Image Generation \& Optimization

- **Tool(s):** DALL-E, Jasper Art, Midjourney, Frase, Unsplash
- Create (or source) featured and in-article images, ensuring each includes keyword-rich alt text.
- **Human Review:** Ensure images are on-brand and meet licensing constraints.[^4]


#### 8. Publishing \& CMS Setup

- **Tool(s):** WordPress, HubSpot, Zapier (automation), Rank Math
- Set proper URL, categories/tags, meta, schema, and internal/external links before publishing live.
- **Human Review:** Do a final live preview, check for formatting and mobile usability.[^17][^15]


#### 9. Rank Tracking \& Monitoring

- **Tool(s):** Semrush, SE Ranking, Google Search Console
- Track ranking, impressions, and clicks for the primary/secondary keywords post-publication.
- **Human Review:** Ongoing review for performance issues; plan updates based on rank/drop.[^6][^18]

***

#### Where Should Human Review Intervene?

- **Critical at:** Keyword selection, outline approval, draft accuracy, final SEO polish, post-publish validation.
- **Optional at:** Outline expansion, FAQ/image prompts, unless high stakes (e.g., YMYL/brand-sensitive content).

***

**Summary Table:**


| Step | Key Tool(s) | Deliverable | Human Review Needed? |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Keyword Research | NeuronWriter | Keyword list \& clusters | Yes |
| Brief \& Outline | Frase, Outranking | SEO-optimized brief | Yes |
| Outline Expansion | ChatGPT, Copy.ai | H1-H3 outline | Optional |
| Draft Generation | Surfer, Jasper | Full article draft | Yes |
| SEO Optimization | Clearscope, Rank Math | Optimized article | Yes |
| Meta/Schema/FAQs | Rank Math, GetGenie | Meta tags/schema/FAQs | Yes |
| Imagery | DALL-E, Unsplash | Featured/section images | Yes |
| Publishing | WordPress | Live article | Yes |
| Rank Tracking | Semrush, GSC | Performance data | Ongoing |


***

This workflow ensures SEO-driven, high-quality content and saves manual work while keeping your site competitive and compliant with Google’s latest content standards.[^1][^2][^3][^8]
